You made it off Mars!

Your ship can fly now. Make fuel cells (4 ice + 1 purple crystal) and pick a world on the star map. Or land again and fight the waves at the Wave beacon.

Wave beacon

Light the beacon to call the next wave. Beat all creatures to win coins and a chest. Every 10 waves a big one comes. Wave 50 is the dragon.

Forge

Weapons and shields. You keep them on every world.

Potion table

Each potion needs a Bottle (make them in your bag). Hold a potion and Use to drink it. You get the bottle back.

Camp stove

Cooked food fills you up much more than raw food. Grow potatoes, beans and lettuce on Farm soil.

Storage box
